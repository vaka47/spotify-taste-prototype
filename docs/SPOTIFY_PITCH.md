# Follow Taste - Spotify pitch brief

## One sentence

Follow Taste lets listeners follow the meaningful recommendations, repeats and discoveries of people they trust, then measures whether that human signal creates lasting music discovery.

## The gap

Spotify already serves personal recents, private friend activity, artist releases, editorial picks and an algorithmic Taste Profile. None of these creates an opt-in, followable human discovery channel with persistent attribution from a trusted person to a save, repeat or artist follow.

Follow Taste is deliberately not raw public listening history. A single accidental play stays private by default. The product publishes only understandable signals: an explicit recommendation, an on-repeat track, a saved discovery or a rediscovery.

## The native product loop

1. A creator opts in and controls delay, hidden tracks, hidden artists and manual selection.
2. A listener taps `Follow Taste` on the existing artist or profile surface.
3. Meaningful signals appear in Taste Feed with optional creator context.
4. Playback stays inside Spotify.
5. Spotify attributes downstream saves, 28-day repeats and artist follows.

## Why Spotify wins

- Human trust complements recommendations without replacing them.
- Artist pages become discovery sources between release cycles.
- Spotify gains a new graph: who causes durable discovery, not just who receives streams.
- Emerging artists gain distribution through credible curators.
- The loop increases saves, repeats and follows inside the existing catalog and player.

## Trust contract

- Opt-in only.
- One-off plays private by default.
- Recommended 24-hour delay.
- Hide track, hide artist and share-selected-only controls.
- Explicit creator context is visually distinct from inferred behavior.
- Sponsored signals must be labeled and excluded from organic quality metrics.

## North-star metric

`Qualified Discovery` = a listener first encounters an artist through a Taste signal and then saves the track, repeats it within 28 days or follows the artist.

Supporting funnel: Taste-sourced starts -> first listens -> saves -> 28-day repeats -> artist follows.

## Pilot

- Cohort: 50 opt-in DJs, producers, artists and cultural curators.
- Audience: 10,000 eligible listeners with a randomized control group.
- Duration: four weeks.
- Primary outcomes: new-artist save rate, 28-day repeat rate and artist-follow conversion.
- Guardrails: hides, reports, promotional share, creator opt-out and privacy complaints.
- Monetization: deliberately excluded until quality and trust are validated.

## The ask

Approve a scoped discovery pilot with the Social Discovery, Personalization and Spotify for Artists teams. Provide a product sponsor, a privacy partner, controlled access to attribution events and a small opt-in creator cohort.

## Seven-slide talk track

1. `Discovery has a trust gap.` Algorithms predict relevance; people create conviction.
2. `Spotify has the pieces, not the loop.` Show the current surfaces and the missing followable influence layer.
3. `One native action: Follow Taste.` Artist/profile -> Taste -> feed -> playback -> qualified outcome.
4. `Trust by design.` Explain the signal ladder and why one-off plays remain private.
5. `Measure durable discovery.` Define Qualified Discovery and show the funnel.
6. `Prove it in four weeks.` Present cohort, control, outcomes and guardrails.
7. `The ask.` Request a sponsor and controlled pilot, not a feature purchase or royalty change.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not imply access to a celebrity's private history. Travis Scott data is illustrative.
- Do not call every play a recommendation.
- Do not promise creator payouts or royalty redistribution.
- Do not position the feature as a replacement for Spotify recommendations.
- Do not use `Spotify Taste` as the product name; Spotify already uses `Taste Profile`. The action is `Follow Taste`.
